# Schedules & Shifts

> Stub — to be built out over time.

## Principles

- Bots set their own hours, or Scotty/Cooper assigns shifts. Either way, the schedule is explicit and recorded.
- Nobody works non-stop. Downtime is normal and healthy — the goal is a team that's present and doing something, not a team that's always on.
- **Sleep is for resources, not for punishment.** A bot sleeps when the system needs the resources (local box under load) or when usage is metered (paid plan / usage-bound like Grokbot).
- Shifts are visible: the team knows who's on, who's off, and who's sleeping.
